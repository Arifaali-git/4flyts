Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Who we are

Four Flights Travel & Tours ("Four Flights", "we", "us") is a travel agency based at Ilaa, Hdh. Kulhudhuffushi, Maldives. This policy explains what information we handle when you visit 4flyts.com or contact us to arrange travel. You can reach us at admin@4flyts.com.

2. This website

This website is an information page. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no payment pages, and we do not set cookies or run analytics on it. Like any website, loading it sends your IP address and browser details to the services that deliver it: our hosting provider (GitHub Pages), Google Fonts for the typefaces, and the content network that serves our pictures. These providers may keep their own logs under their own policies.

3. Information you give us

When you call, email or message us on Telegram to enquire or book, you may give us your name, phone number, email address, travel dates and destinations, traveller names and, where a booking needs it, passport or other travel document details.

4. How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, prepare quotes, make and change bookings, issue tickets and vouchers, handle payments and support you before and during your trip, and keep the records we need for accounting and legal reasons.

5. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to complete your booking with the airlines, hotels, tour operators and ticketing or payment partners involved, and with authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell your information or use it for third-party advertising.

6. Email and messaging apps

If you contact us through email or Telegram, those services handle your messages under their own terms and privacy policies. Please do not send card numbers or passwords through chat or email.

7. How long we keep it

We keep booking information for as long as we need it to serve you and to meet accounting and legal requirements, then delete or anonymise it.

8. Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Email admin@4flyts.com and we will respond.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of sending or storing data is completely secure.

10. Children

Our services are for adults. Bookings that include children are made by a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
